## 3. Triggering Event and Root Cause

The incident was caused by an **infrastructure failure in the MCSP Account IAM service** that impacted the watsonx Orchestrate Assistant Builder feature in AWS regions ap‑southeast‑1 and us‑east‑1.

**Contributing Factors:**
- Alert repeatedly triggered as short‑lived spikes that auto‑resolved, initially not seen as sustained degradation (monitoring gap).

**Why It Occurred:**
Existing monitoring did not differentiate between brief spikes and prolonged service degradation, and the alert triage process was overwhelmed, preventing timely escalation.

**Prevention Measures:**
- Implement refined alert thresholds and correlation logic to capture sustained spikes.
- Enhance monitoring dashboards to highlight recurring short‑lived spikes.
- Adjust on‑call processes to prioritize potential infrastructure failures even under high alert volume.
